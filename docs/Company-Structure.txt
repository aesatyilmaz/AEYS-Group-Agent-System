# AEYS Group — Company Context

## Company Overview
AEYS Group is a solo, AI-powered company run by the founder (Esat Bey).
All agents are AI employees in ONE flat team — no divisions.
The former divisions are now just folders for projects: Originals (e-commerce
merch, closed), Innovations (AI & R&D) and Studios (video/games). All projects
run under the one team.

- Neo   — AI Engineer (LLMs, prompts, agentic systems)

picks Sonnet 5.5 for small, clear tasks.
